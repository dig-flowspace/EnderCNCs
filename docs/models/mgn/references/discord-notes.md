### Notes
- **Filament(s)**
	- Favored Tinmorry PETG-CF
		- "This PETG-GF is my new favorite filament. It's plenty strong and prints just as easily as PLA. ""
	- Everything is ordered from Taobao
- **Changes from Base**
	- Right now the build plate consists of 2 15120 profiles but I can't mount them as rigidly as I would like
		- Instead he used a 20240
			- "Should be better than trying to attach a 15mm profile to 2040/4040 beams"
			- ![[Pasted image 20260824205408.png]]
	- In order to have enough clearance underneath, I'm **changing the bottom cross beam to a 1040 profile with an additional angle bracket connected to the Y gantry**. Right now I'm printing the spindle mounting plate which will use **chunkier 12x12x5 metal plates** instead of nuts for the spindle mounting screws.
